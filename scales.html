Pre-Assessment Questionnaires

Before your upcoming assessment, please complete all five questionnaires below. They help your clinician understand your symptoms and experiences more fully before the session begins.

Each questionnaire is short and straightforward. Please answer honestly — there are no right or wrong answers. Your responses go directly and securely to our clinical team.

Please complete all five questionnaires at least 48 hours before your assessment.
Your clinician will review your responses before the session. If you have any difficulty, contact us at booking@ammcoreassessments.co.uk.
