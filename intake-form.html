Section 8

Symptom Screening

Rate how often you have experienced each of the following over the past 6 months. There are no right or wrong answers.

This is a standardised screening tool used by clinicians to understand your experience before your assessment. Your answers will be reviewed alongside the rest of your form.

Attention & Focus

How often do you have trouble wrapping up the final details of a project once the challenging parts have been done?
How often do you have difficulty getting things in order when you have to do a task that requires organisation?
How often do you have problems remembering appointments or obligations?
When you have a task that requires a lot of thought, how often do you avoid or delay getting started?

Activity & Impulse Control

How often do you fidget or squirm with your hands or feet when you have to sit down for a long time?
How often do you feel overly active and compelled to do things, like you were driven by a motor?

Section 9

Supporting Documents

Optional, but strongly recommended.

Why does this matter?

Providing this information helps us make the most accurate and clinically robust assessment possible. Documents are optional — you will not be refused an assessment for not providing them — but they significantly strengthen the evidence base available to your clinician.

📝 How to send your documents

Please email any supporting documents to:

booking@ammcoreassessments.co.uk

Use your full name as the email subject so we can match it to your form.

We can accept any of the following:

  • GP records / medical history letters
  • School reports Highly Recommended
  • Parent or caregiver written account Highly Recommended
  • Previous ADHD / autism / psychiatric reports
  • Work-related evidence of impairment (HR letters, occupational health reports)

🔒 All documents are handled securely and used solely for the purposes of your clinical assessment in accordance with our Privacy Policy and UK GDPR.

Section 10

Risk & Safety

These questions help ensure your safety and allow us to direct you to the right support.

⚠️ Immediate support is available

If you are in crisis or immediate danger, please contact one of the following right away:

  • Call 999 or 112 — emergency services
  • Go to your nearest A&E department
  • Call Samaritans: 116 123 — free, 24/7
  • Text SHOUT to 85258 — free crisis text line

We are not able to proceed with an assessment where there is active risk to life. Please speak to your GP or seek emergency support before continuing with a booking.
