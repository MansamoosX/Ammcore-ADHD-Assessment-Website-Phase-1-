How to send this as an email

  1. 1 Open Gmail and compose a new email to the clinician's email address
  2. 2 Set subject line: Welcome to Ammcore Assessments — Your Clinician Onboarding Pack
  3. 3 Click the three-dot menu (⋮) at the bottom of the compose window and select "Insert from Drive" or paste the URL of this page into the email body. Alternatively, copy-paste the text content below and add the links manually.
  4. 4 Replace [Clinician Name] with the clinician's first name before sending
  5. 5 Send from booking@ammcoreassessments.co.uk

Tip — easiest method: Forward this page's URL (https://ammcoreassessments.co.uk/clinician-onboarding-email.html) as a link directly in your Gmail. The clinician can open it in their browser and access all documents from one page.
