Section 1

Purpose of This Document

This guide sets out the standardised framework for conducting ADHD triage appointments at Ammcore Clinical Assessments. It covers the clinical parameters used to determine patient suitability, and provides ready-to-use outcome email templates for both suitable and not suitable decisions.

All clinicians are expected to apply this framework consistently to ensure every patient receives a fair, evidence-based, and compassionate outcome.

Important: The triage session is a clinical screening appointment, not a diagnostic assessment. Its purpose is to determine whether a full ADHD diagnostic assessment is the appropriate next step for this patient at this time.

Section 3

Pre-Triage Clinician Review

Before each triage session, the clinician must review the patient's completed intake form. The following fields are of highest clinical relevance:

Intake SectionWhat to Look For
Section 4 — Childhood HistoryEvidence of symptoms before age 12. This is essential for ADHD diagnosis under DSM-5/ICD-11. Note if absent — may need collateral.
Section 5 — SymptomsFrequency and pattern of inattention and hyperactivity/impulsivity symptoms in current daily life.
Section 6 — Functional ImpactEvidence of impairment across multiple settings (work, relationships, daily life). Required for diagnosis.
Section 7 — Mental HealthPresence of anxiety, depression, trauma, or other conditions that may better explain the presentation.
Section 8 — ASRS ScoreAuto-calculated score (0–6). Score ≥ 4 suggests high symptom likelihood. Score provided in Formspree submission.
Section 9 — Supporting DocumentsCheck booking@ inbox for documents emailed by patient. Note if absent — request collateral during triage.
Section 10 — Risk & SafetyAny active risk flags. If HIGH RISK flag is present in email, follow risk escalation protocol before proceeding.
Section 4

Suitability Decision Parameters

The triage outcome must be based on a holistic clinical judgement across all available information. The following indicators should guide — but not mechanically determine — the decision.

Indicators Supporting NOT SUITABLE

  • Symptoms are better explained by another condition (anxiety, depression, PTSD, sleep disorder)
  • No evidence of childhood onset and no corroborating collateral available
  • Active, unmanaged risk to life — defer until patient has appropriate crisis support in place
  • Patient is currently under a crisis team or acute mental health service — defer to existing service
  • Presentation does not meet clinical threshold for ADHD diagnostic assessment
  • Diagnosis would rely entirely on self-report with no corroboration and significant differential indicators present

Indicators Supporting SUITABLE

  • ASRS score ≥ 4 with consistent symptom endorsement across intake form
  • Childhood onset evidenced or strongly suggested by patient history
  • Functional impairment documented across multiple settings
  • Symptoms not fully explained by differential diagnosis
  • Collateral evidence available or obtainable to corroborate self-report
  • No active unmanaged risk that would preclude assessment

Clinical note on collateral: Where supporting documents are absent, the clinician should use the triage session to request collateral — school reports, parent/caregiver account, or GP letter. If self-report is the only available evidence and significant differential indicators are present, document this and consider deferring the assessment until collateral is obtained.

Timeframe for Outcome Communication

The outcome email must be sent to the patient within 24 hours of the triage appointment. Do not leave patients waiting beyond this window.

Section 6

Record Keeping

Following each triage, the clinician must record the following:

  • Patient name and date of triage
  • Outcome decision (suitable / not suitable)
  • Brief clinical rationale (2–3 sentences minimum)
  • ASRS score from intake form
  • Whether collateral was available, requested, or absent
  • Any risk factors identified and action taken
  • Date and time outcome email was sent

Data protection: All patient records must be handled in accordance with UK GDPR and our Privacy Policy. Patient data must not be stored on personal devices. Contact the service lead if you are unsure about data handling procedures.
