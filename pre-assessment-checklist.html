Patient Details

A

Administrative — Before the Session

Full assessment payment confirmed Required
Verify the £600 (Standard) or £800 (Express) balance has been received in Stripe before proceeding. Do not commence the assessment if payment has not cleared.
Intake form submitted Required
Confirm the patient's intake form has been received via Formspree (booking@ammcoreassessments.co.uk). The assessment cannot proceed without this.
Clinical disclaimer read and signed Required
Confirm the patient has read, signed, and returned the clinical disclaimer. If not received, obtain verbal confirmation at the start of the session and document below.
Identity verified Required
Patient must confirm their identity at the start of the session by showing photo ID (passport, driving licence, or equivalent) to the camera.
B

Supporting Documents

GP records / referral letter Recommended
Check booking@ammcoreassessments.co.uk for emailed documents. GP records or referral letter provide clinical context and corroborate presenting history.
School reports Recommended
School reports provide strong evidence of childhood symptom onset — a key DSM-5 criterion. Highly valuable if available.
Informant / collateral information Recommended
Parent, partner, or carer input significantly strengthens the assessment. Check if an informant form or email has been received from a third party.
Previous assessments or psychiatric reports
Any prior ADHD, ASD, or mental health assessment reports that may affect the clinical picture or diagnostic outcome.
Work / occupational evidence
Performance reviews, HR correspondence, or occupational health reports evidencing functional impairment in the workplace.
C

Intake Form Review — Before the Session

ASRS score reviewed Required
Review the patient's ASRS v1.1 score from the Formspree submission. Score ≥4 = HIGH. Note the score and flag before the session begins.
Pre-assessment rating scales received & reviewed Required
Confirm the patient has submitted all five questionnaires via the scales portal (ammcoreassessments.co.uk/scales.html). Check booking@ammcoreassessments.co.uk inbox for Formspree submissions. Required: WURS-25 · AQ-10 · BDI · BAI · WFIRS. Note each score below before the session begins.
Risk & safety responses reviewed Risk Item
Check the patient's responses to the Risk & Safety section of the intake form. Note any disclosures of current or historical suicidal ideation, self-harm, or harm to others.
Presenting history and background read
Familiarise yourself with the patient's self-reported history, developmental background, and main concerns prior to the session. Do not go in cold.
Current medications and health conditions noted
Note any medications or health conditions that may affect presentation, contraindicate treatment, or require consideration in the report.
D

Session Setup

Video platform tested and ready Required
Confirm Google Meet link is active, camera and microphone are functioning, and you are in a quiet, private location.
DIVA 5.0 interview guide open and ready Required
Ensure the structured clinical interview guide (DIVA 5.0) is open and accessible before starting. Do not conduct the interview from memory.
Diagnostic report form open and ready
Open the Ammcore ADHD Diagnostic Report form before starting the session so you can capture notes in real time.
90 minutes blocked — no interruptions
Confirm your calendar is clear for the full assessment duration. The session should not be interrupted or cut short.
E

Risk & Escalation — Review Before Every Session

No current suicidal ideation disclosed Risk Item
If the patient disclosed current suicidal ideation in the intake form or at any point during the session, do not proceed. Follow the risk escalation protocol immediately.
No current self-harm disclosed Risk Item
If the patient disclosed current self-harm, assess severity and frequency. Escalate to the clinical lead before concluding the session. Provide crisis resources directly to the patient.
No safeguarding concerns identified Risk Item
Consider vulnerability, capacity, and any concerns regarding the welfare of the patient or others. Escalate immediately if a safeguarding concern arises.
Clinical lead contact details to hand
Ensure you have the clinical lead's contact details available before the session begins in case escalation is required during the assessment.
F

Post-Assessment — After the Session

Diagnostic report drafted
Complete the Ammcore ADHD Diagnostic Report form while the session is fresh. Aim to draft within 24 hours of the assessment.
Report submitted for consultant review Required
All reports must be reviewed by a consultant clinician before being sent to the patient. Do not send the report directly without this step.
GP letter — if contact details available
If the patient's GP details are known, a GP letter should be included with or following the report. If GP details are not held, the patient should be asked to either provide their GP's email so we can send it on their behalf, or to forward the report to their GP themselves.
Record keeping completed
Confirm all session notes, rating scale scores, and the final report have been stored securely in accordance with UK GDPR and Ammcore's data retention policy.
