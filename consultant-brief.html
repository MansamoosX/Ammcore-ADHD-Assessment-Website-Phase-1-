Consultant Opportunity Brief

Private ADHD Diagnostic Assessment Service · 2026

Ammcore Clinical Assessments is a private adult ADHD diagnostic service operating under Ammcore Consulting Ltd. We provide structured, consultant-reviewed assessments delivered entirely online, with a clear clinical framework and a fully managed administrative pipeline. We are seeking experienced consultant psychiatrists to join our clinical team on a flexible, per-assessment basis.

The Service

What We Offer

  • Private adult ADHD diagnostic assessments
  • Structured 90-minute clinical interviews
  • DSM-5 / DIVA 5.0 clinical framework
  • Pre-submitted validated rating scales (ASRS, WURS-25, WFIRS, AQ-10, BDI, BAI)
  • Standard pathway — report within 7 working days
  • Express pathway — report within 3 working days
  • All sessions conducted via secure video platform
Admin — Fully Managed

What You Don't Handle

  • All patient communication and coordination
  • Booking, scheduling, and payment collection
  • Intake forms and pre-assessment documentation
  • Report formatting and delivery to patients
  • GP correspondence coordination
  • Triage and suitability screening
Clinical Roles

Two Ways to Work With Us

Assessing Clinician
Conducts the structured 90-minute clinical interview with the patient. Completes the diagnostic report form immediately post-session using our pre-built documentation. Report is then submitted for consultant review before delivery.
Consultant Reviewer
Reviews completed diagnostic reports submitted by the assessing clinician. Signs off on clinical accuracy and diagnostic conclusions before the report is released to the patient. No direct patient contact required.
