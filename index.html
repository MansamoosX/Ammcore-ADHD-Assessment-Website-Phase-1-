The Process

How It Works

From booking to receiving your report, we've designed every step to be straightforward, supportive, and transparent.

1

Pay & Book Triage

Pay the £99 non-refundable booking fee, then book your Saturday triage slot via our secure booking system. You will receive a confirmation email with a link to your intake form.

2

Complete Intake Form

Complete your patient intake form at least 24 hours before your triage. This must be submitted in advance — your triage appointment cannot proceed without it.

3

Saturday Triage

Attend a short 15–20 minute screening call on Saturday. Your clinician will confirm suitability and book your Sunday full assessment slot on the call.

4

Sunday Assessment

Your full structured clinical interview takes place on Sunday — remotely via secure video. Your clinician uses validated ADHD diagnostic tools and a thorough developmental history.

5

Receive Your Report

Your comprehensive written report is delivered securely — within 7 working days (Standard) or 3 working days (Express) — summarising findings, diagnostic outcome, and tailored recommendations.

The Reality of NHS Waiting Times

Most people wait years for an NHS assessment.

NHS waiting lists for adult ADHD assessments in England currently run between 2 and 7 years in many areas — and are growing. For many people, that means years of struggling at work, in relationships, and with daily life without any answers or support.

A private assessment with Ammcore Clinical Assessments can typically be arranged within weeks — giving you clarity, a formal report, and a path forward without the prolonged wait.
