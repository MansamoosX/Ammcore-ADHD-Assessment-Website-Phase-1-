The Consultant

A suitably qualified and registered clinician whose details are confirmed by signature in Section 11 of this Agreement.

1. Introduction

This Agreement sets out the terms under which the Consultant will provide professional clinical services to Ammcore Clinical Assessments. Both parties agree to be bound by these terms upon signing.

2. Purpose

The Consultant will provide professional oversight, review, and consultation on clinical assessments conducted by Ammcore Clinical Assessments. This may include:

  • Reviewing structured ADHD and autism assessment reports
  • Providing professional guidance on clinical interpretation and report drafting
  • Co-signing or endorsing reports where applicable
  • Advising on complex cases, clinical queries, or escalations

3. Scope of Work

The Consultant agrees to:

  • Conduct all reviews solely within their professional competence and registration scope
  • Provide timely feedback to ensure assessment reports are accurate and clinically sound
  • Apply recognised diagnostic frameworks including DSM-5, ICD-11, and appropriate assessment tools (DIVA, ADOS, ADI-R) where relevant
  • Operate on a flexible, case-by-case basis as agreed with Ammcore Clinical Assessments

4. Responsibilities

Consultant Responsibilities:

  • Maintain current professional registration and comply with all applicable regulatory body requirements
  • Maintain strict confidentiality of all patient information accessed in the course of this Agreement
  • Notify Ammcore Clinical Assessments promptly of any conflicts of interest, limitations, or changes to registration status
  • Maintain their own professional indemnity insurance adequate for the scope of services provided

Ammcore Clinical Assessments Responsibilities:

  • Provide accurate assessment materials, reports, and relevant background information for review
  • Ensure secure methods of data transfer and communication
  • Compensate the Consultant in accordance with Section 6

5. Confidentiality & Data Protection

  • Both parties shall comply with UK GDPR and all applicable professional confidentiality obligations
  • The Consultant's access to patient data is strictly limited to clinical review purposes under this Agreement
  • The Consultant shall not disclose, copy, or retain identifiable patient information beyond what is required for the specific review
  • This confidentiality obligation survives the termination of this Agreement

6. Fees & Payment

The Consultant shall be remunerated per completed assessment at the following rates:

  • Assessing Clinician — Standard pathway: £200 per assessment
  • Assessing Clinician — Express pathway: £225 per assessment
  • Consultant Reviewer — Standard pathway: £300 per assessment
  • Consultant Reviewer — Express pathway: £325 per assessment
  • Invoices shall be submitted by the Consultant and paid within 14 working days of receipt
  • No additional expenses or reimbursements shall be paid unless expressly agreed in writing in advance
  • The Consultant is responsible for their own tax and National Insurance obligations as an independent contractor
  • Rates may be reviewed by mutual written agreement with a minimum of 4 weeks' notice

7. Independent Contractor Status

The Consultant is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee of Ammcore Consulting Ltd. Nothing in this Agreement shall create or imply a relationship of employment, agency, or partnership between the parties.

8. Indemnity

  • The Consultant shall maintain professional indemnity insurance sufficient to cover the scope of services outlined in this Agreement and shall provide evidence of such insurance upon request
  • Ammcore Consulting Ltd's company indemnity covers actions conducted under its instruction; the Consultant remains personally responsible for their own professional conduct and clinical decisions

9. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 4 weeks' written notice to the other party.

  • Termination does not extinguish the obligation to maintain confidentiality over existing patient information
  • Any outstanding reviews or reports at the point of termination shall be completed or appropriately handed over, as agreed between the parties

10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes arising from or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
