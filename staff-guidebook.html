Welcome

Welcome to Ammcore Clinical Assessments. We are glad to have you as part of our clinical team.

Ammcore Clinical Assessments was founded on a simple belief: that people who need clarity about their mental health should not have to wait years to get it. We provide private, clinician-led ADHD diagnostic assessments that are rigorous, compassionate, and accessible. Every patient who comes to us has made a significant decision to invest in understanding themselves better — and it is our job to honour that.

This guidebook exists to ensure that every clinician at Ammcore delivers to the same standard. It is not optional reading — it is the foundation of how we operate. Please read it in full before conducting your first assessment, and return to it whenever you need guidance.

Our Promise to Every Patient Every patient receives the same high standard of clinical care — regardless of which clinician conducts their assessment, which service they have purchased, or what their background is.

Your Role as a Clinician

As an Ammcore clinician, your role is to conduct thorough, standardised ADHD diagnostic assessments and produce clear, accurate written reports. You are the patient's primary clinical contact during the assessment and your conduct, care, and clinical judgement directly reflects the Ammcore standard.

What You Are Responsible For

  • Reviewing all pre-assessment documentation before each session
  • Completing the Pre-Assessment Clinician Checklist before every assessment
  • Conducting the assessment using the Structured Clinical Interview Guide
  • Administering and recording all required rating scales
  • Completing the ADHD Diagnostic Report form following each session
  • Submitting the report for consultant review — never sending directly to the patient
  • Escalating any risk concerns immediately during or after the session
  • Maintaining patient confidentiality at all times

What You Are Not Responsible For

  • Chasing or confirming patient payments — this is handled by admin
  • Sending the final report to the patient — this is handled after consultant sign-off
  • Booking or rescheduling patient appointments — patients manage this via Calendly
  • Marketing, patient acquisition, or website content
Important If you are ever unsure whether something falls within your remit, contact the clinical lead before acting. It is always better to ask than to assume.

Clinical Standards

The following standards are non-negotiable. Every assessment conducted at Ammcore must meet all of them, without exception.

Assessment Standards

  • Use the structured interview guide. Do not conduct assessments from memory or improvise the structure. The guide exists to ensure every patient is assessed to the same standard.
  • Administer all required rating scales. DIVA 5.0, ASRS v1.1, WURS-25, WFIRS, AQ-10, BDI, and BAI are required for every assessment. Do not omit any without clinical justification documented in the report.
  • Identity must be verified. Every patient must show photo ID before the assessment begins. No exceptions.
  • Consent must be confirmed. Verbal consent must be obtained and recorded even if the written disclaimer has been signed.
  • 90 minutes minimum. Assessments must not be rushed. If more time is needed, take it. If the patient is significantly late, contact admin before proceeding.

Report Standards

  • Use the Ammcore Diagnostic Report form. Do not create your own format. The standardised form ensures consistency across all reports.
  • Draft within 24 hours. Complete your report draft while the session is fresh. Memory fades quickly — do not leave it.
  • Be specific and evidence-based. Every diagnostic conclusion must be supported by the patient's own words and the rating scale scores. Avoid vague generalisations.
  • Never send directly to the patient. All reports must be reviewed by a consultant clinician before delivery. This is a mandatory quality and safety control.
  • Meet the deadline. Standard reports within 7 working days. Express reports within 3 working days from the date of assessment.

Conduct Standards

  • Maintain a professional, neutral, and compassionate tone throughout every session
  • Do not express personal opinions about the patient's situation, lifestyle, or choices
  • Do not speculate about diagnoses before the assessment is complete
  • Do not make promises about outcomes — the report will speak for itself
  • Do not communicate with patients outside of the agreed channels
Zero Tolerance Breaches of clinical standards, confidentiality, or professional conduct will be treated as serious matters and may result in the immediate termination of your engagement with Ammcore Clinical Assessments.

Risk & Escalation Policy

Patient safety is the highest priority at Ammcore Clinical Assessments. The following policy must be followed without exception whenever a risk concern arises.

Risk Levels

Level Presentation Immediate Action
HIGH Current suicidal ideation with intent or plan; active self-harm; immediate risk to others Stop the session. Provide emergency numbers immediately. Contact clinical lead. Do not leave the patient alone on screen if risk is immediate.
MEDIUM Historical suicidal ideation or self-harm; passive thoughts of death without intent; significant distress Continue with care. Provide crisis resources at the end of the session. Document fully. Notify clinical lead same day.
LOW No active risk. Historical disclosures that are resolved and stable. Document in report. No immediate escalation required.

What to Say to a Patient in Crisis

"I can hear that you're going through something very difficult right now, and I want to make sure you're safe. Before we go any further, I need to make sure you have the right support available to you. Please contact NHS 111 and select the mental health option — they can help you right now. If you feel you are in immediate danger, please call 999 or go to your nearest A&E. Would it be okay if I stayed on the call with you for a moment while you do that?"

Crisis Resources — Provide to Patient

  • NHS 111 — Call 111, select mental health option (24/7)
  • 999 / A&E — Immediate life-threatening emergencies
  • Samaritans — 116 123 (free, 24/7)
  • Crisis text line — Text SHOUT to 85258

Escalation Steps

  1. Assess the risk level using the table above.
  2. Provide the patient with appropriate crisis resources.
  3. Contact the clinical lead immediately — by phone if HIGH risk, by email if MEDIUM risk.
  4. Document the full disclosure, your assessment, and every action taken in the diagnostic report and checklist.
  5. Do not proceed with the assessment until the clinical lead has been consulted on HIGH risk cases.
  6. Follow up with the clinical lead after the session to confirm the escalation has been logged.

Safeguarding

If at any point during an assessment you identify a safeguarding concern — regarding the patient or any other person, including children — you must escalate immediately to the clinical lead. Do not wait until the end of the session. Safeguarding concerns take priority over the assessment.

Non-Negotiable Risk escalation is never optional. If you are uncertain whether something constitutes a risk, escalate. It is always better to escalate unnecessarily than to miss a genuine concern.

Data Protection & Confidentiality

All patient data is subject to UK GDPR. As a clinician working with Ammcore, you are a data processor and are legally required to handle patient information in accordance with data protection law.

Your Obligations

  • Only access patient data that is necessary for the assessment you are conducting
  • Do not store patient data on personal devices, personal email accounts, or personal cloud storage
  • Do not share patient data with anyone other than the clinical lead and consultant reviewer
  • Do not discuss patient cases in any setting where you could be overheard
  • Report any suspected data breach to the clinical lead immediately
  • Use only Ammcore-approved systems for storing or transmitting patient data

Confidentiality

Everything a patient shares during an assessment is confidential. There are three exceptions where confidentiality may be broken:

  • The patient provides consent for information to be shared
  • There is a serious and immediate risk of harm to the patient or another person
  • You are required to disclose information by law

In all three cases, you must inform the clinical lead before taking action.

Report Storage Completed reports must be submitted via the agreed secure channel to admin. Do not email final reports from personal accounts. Do not retain copies of reports on personal devices beyond what is necessary for review purposes.

Professional Conduct

As an Ammcore clinician you represent the organisation in every interaction. The following standards of conduct are expected at all times.

During Assessments

  • Be punctual — join the call on time. If you are delayed, notify admin immediately.
  • Be appropriately presented — treat remote assessments with the same professionalism as in-person appointments.
  • Be in a quiet, private location with no risk of interruption for the full 90 minutes.
  • Do not multitask during the assessment. Give the patient your full attention.
  • Do not eat, drink (other than water), or use your phone during the session.

Boundaries

  • Maintain professional boundaries at all times — do not form personal relationships with patients.
  • Do not accept gifts, payments, or any other benefit from patients directly.
  • If a patient contacts you personally outside of Ammcore channels, redirect them to booking@ammcoreassessments.co.uk and notify admin.
  • Do not discuss your personal life, opinions, or circumstances with patients.

Self-Care

Clinical work, particularly with patients in distress, can be emotionally demanding. If you find that a case or a session has affected you, please reach out to the clinical lead. Supervision and support are available. You cannot deliver good clinical care if you are not well yourself.

Supervision Regular clinical supervision is part of maintaining your professional standards. If you have concerns about a case, a patient, or your own wellbeing, raise them with the clinical lead. No concern is too small.
